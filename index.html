About

From 2012 to 2021 I served as Chief Rabbi of Finland, leading a national community of about 1,300 members, its staff and its budget, and representing it to the government, the media and interfaith partners. That work taught me to explain complex topics clearly and to work with many kinds of stakeholders.

In 2021 I turned to crypto research. I studied the field on my own, started advising private clients, and after moving to Israel in 2022 joined dWallet Labs as a product researcher, writing research briefs on zero-knowledge proofs, MPC, modular chains and cross-chain bridges. At Myntra Capital I worked as a crypto and equities analyst and published seven byline articles.

Now I am in the GenAI & ML bootcamp at Developers Institute (graduating January 2027), learning Python, data analysis, machine learning, LLMs and AI agents. My finance degree, research background and years of leadership are what I bring to AI teams, especially in crypto, fintech and AI-first startups.
